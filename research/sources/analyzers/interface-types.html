Explanation of interface types:
4
GraphicalAn application with a graphical interface, generally easy to use.
5
WebsiteRuns in a browser and often has at least a partial graphical interface.
6
Command lineRuns in a terminal and takes commands in plain text.
7
ScriptThe most difficult to use. Runs directly as code with no interface. May produce outputs as text or files.
8
Special/OtherRequires special explanation.
