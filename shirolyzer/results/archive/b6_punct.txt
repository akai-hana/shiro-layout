   only -0.014 punct-axis -> cfgE's letters are already near-optimal once
   punct mass is priced (English placement robust).

   (they evict c/m or k/d and leave them displaced) -> the Kuntem fix cannot
   be done as a bare punct swap; it needs the joint re-search.

   (-0.087 vs cfgE), relocating '.' to Q (left ring top), ';' to the pinky
   bottom (rare char), '/' to B (left index), ',' stays right ring. The
   s. pinky-SFB is killed by making s.-type pairs cross-hand alternation.

   real and quantified: -0.087 punct-axis for +0.070 letter-axis. Ship as
   optional cfgE-punct variant only if the '.'-on-Q reach (human judgment,
   not priced beyond key effort) is acceptable; primary cfgE unchanged.
